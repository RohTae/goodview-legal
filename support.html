Frequently Asked Questions

How does GoodView find scenic views near me?

GoodView uses your device's GPS location to display scenic viewpoints nearby. All viewpoint data is sourced from OpenStreetMap's community-verified database, covering 40,000+ locations across the United States.

Do I need an account to use GoodView?

No. You can browse the map, view details, and get directions without an account. An account is only needed to bookmark favorites, submit reviews, or contribute new scenic views.

Why is a viewpoint missing or incorrect?

Our data comes from OpenStreetMap and may not include every location. If you find a missing or incorrect viewpoint, you can submit a correction through the app using the "Suggest Edit" feature, or contact us directly.

How do I report inappropriate content?

Tap the report button on any review or photo to flag it. Our team reviews all reports and takes action promptly.

Is GoodView free?

Yes. GoodView is free to download and use. The app includes ads to support development.

What permissions does GoodView need?

GoodView only requests location access (while using the app) to show nearby scenic views. No other permissions are required.
