Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2, 2026

GoodView ("we", "our", or "the app") is a scenic view discovery app. This policy describes how we handle your information.

1. Information We Collect

Location Data. When you grant permission, we access your device's GPS coordinates to show scenic views near you. Location data is sent to our server solely to query nearby viewpoints and is not stored or linked to any identity.

No Account Required. GoodView v0.1 does not require sign-up. We do not collect your name, email, or any personal identifiers.

Local Bookmarks. Saved bookmarks are stored only on your device using local storage. We do not have access to your bookmarks.

2. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — Database hosting for scenic view data. Your GPS coordinates are sent as query parameters; no personal data is stored.
  • Mapbox — Map rendering. Subject to Mapbox Privacy Policy.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone)
  • Usage analytics or tracking
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Contacts, photos, or other device data

4. Data Retention

We do not store any user-specific data on our servers. Scenic view data (sourced from OpenStreetMap and public datasets) is stored in our database but is not linked to individual users.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
